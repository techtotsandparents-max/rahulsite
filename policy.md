# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** April 2026

At **Rahul Tripathi** (operating rahul.tripathies.com and associated digital properties, collectively referred to as "we", "us", or "our"), we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website at [rahul.tripathies.com](https://rahul.tripathies.com) or interact with our content, tools, and services.

By accessing or using our website, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with our policies and practices, please do not use the site.

---

### 1. Information We Collect

We collect information to provide better services to our visitors, readers, and community members:

- **Contact & Communication Information:** When you subscribe to our newsletter, submit a contact message, or reach out for inquiries, we collect your name, email address, and any message details you provide.
- **Account & Authentication Information:** If you authenticate via third-party providers (such as GitHub OAuth or Azure Active Directory), we receive basic public profile information permitted by your authorization settings (such as display name, avatar, and email).
- **Device & Technical Information:** Like most websites, our servers automatically log standard web requests, including your browser type, device type, operating system, IP address, referring URL, and timestamps.
- **Preferences & Local Storage:** We use local storage and cookies strictly for essential functional preferences, such as remembering your **Day (Light) / Night (Dark)** mode preference and audio mute toggles. You can clear this data at any time via your browser settings.
- **Analytics Information:** We may collect aggregated, non-personally identifiable usage data to understand page views, reading engagement, and popular topics to help improve site content. We respect user privacy and honor "Do Not Track" browser signals.

---

### 2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information solely for legitimate purposes, including:

- Delivering blog articles, architectural tutorials, project demonstrations, and travel logs.
- Sending email newsletters and updates you have explicitly subscribed to (you may opt out at any time).
- Responding directly to your inquiries, questions, feedback, and collaboration requests.
- Monitoring site reliability, preventing spam, detecting security threats, and optimizing performance.
- Personalizing your browsing experience (e.g., maintaining your chosen day/night visual theme).

We do **not** sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties or advertisers.

---

### 3. Third-Party Services & Integrations

We may use trusted third-party cloud infrastructure and services to operate this platform:

- **Hosting & Infrastructure:** Azure Web Apps and related secure cloud hosting providers.
- **Authentication Providers:** GitHub OAuth and Microsoft Azure Identity for secure, credential-free logins.
- **Media & Embedded Content:** YouTube video embeds, social profile links, and cloud storage for project assets.

These third-party providers maintain their own independent privacy policies and security standards. We encourage you to review their policies when utilizing external links.

---

### 4. Data Security & Retention

We implement administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, loss, alteration, or misuse:

- HTTPS / TLS encryption on all data transfers across the web.
- Strict security headers (`X-Content-Type-Options`, `X-Frame-Options`, `Strict-Transport-Security`, `Referrer-Policy`).
- Personal data is retained only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this policy, or to comply with applicable legal obligations.

---

### 5. Your Rights and Choices

Depending on your jurisdiction, you have certain rights regarding your personal data:

- **Access & Correction:** You may request details about the personal data we hold about you and request corrections if any information is inaccurate.
- **Deletion:** You may request the deletion of your personal contact information from our subscriber lists.
- **Opt-Out:** You can unsubscribe from newsletter emails at any time by clicking the unsubscribe link at the bottom of our emails or contacting us directly.

### 6. Children's Privacy

Our website and services are intended for software engineers, technology professionals, students, and general tech audiences. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children under 13 years of age.
